Rubayyat Hashmi

Economist

Health & Mental Health Economics · Labour Economics · Applied Microeconomics · Energy & Environmental Economics

I am an applied economist studying how economic disadvantage and labour-market conditions shape health, mental health, wellbeing and inequality. My research uses longitudinal data, applied econometrics and policy evaluation, with particular interests in mental health, disability, labour-market disadvantage and energy poverty.

Publications

  1. Health & mental health · Journal article

    Socioeconomic mental health disparities and income mobility in Australia: A longitudinal factor decomposition analysis

    Hashmi, R., Alam, K., & Gow, J. (2024). Economic Analysis and Policy, 82, 290–302.

    Decomposes socioeconomic mental-health inequality over time and examines the roles of health and income mobility in observed disparities.

    Article DOI

  2. Labour & disability · Journal article

    An investigation of the longitudinal trajectory patterns of health-related quality of life among Australians with disabilities: Explaining disability types and properties

    Hashmi, R., Keating, B. W., Ali, M. A., & Keramat, S. A. (2024). Quality of Life Research, 33(8), 2207–2217.

    Examines longitudinal health-related quality-of-life trajectories among Australians with disability and their relationship with disability and socioeconomic characteristics.

    Article DOI

  3. Energy & environment · Journal article

    Unpacking the mental health effects of energy poverty: Implications of energy poverty metric choice for research and policy

    Hashmi, R., Clair, A., & Baker, E. (2025). Energy Research & Social Science, 125, 104115.

    Shows why the measurement of energy poverty matters for estimating mental-health effects, including the consequences of cumulative exposure.

    Article DOI
