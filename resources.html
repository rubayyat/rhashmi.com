Resources

A curated collection of resources for economics, research methods, policy, publishing and academic work.

This is an evolving collection of websites, tools and readings that I find useful for research and teaching. External resources are grouped by purpose so they are easy to scan and revisit.

Economics & econometrics

  • World Bank BlogsResearch and commentary on economics, development and public policy.
  • Statistics By JimAccessible explanations of statistical concepts and methods.
  • INOMICS — Economics blogsA directory-style starting point for economics commentary and learning.
  • Learn Econometrics FastEconometrics-focused learning resources and blog recommendations.
  • DonskerClass — EconometricsNotes and posts on econometrics and quantitative methods.

Health, mental health & policy

  • Australian Institute of Health and WelfareAustralian health and welfare data, reports and topic collections.
  • Australian Parliamentary LibraryResearch and policy material across major Australian public-policy topics.
  • Disability Royal Commission — PublicationsReports and evidence on disability, inclusion and institutional responses.
  • Australian Mental Health Think TankResearch, policy discussion and resources on mental health in Australia.
  • The Conversation — Health PolicyResearch-informed public commentary on health policy.
  • European Observatory — Health Systems ReviewsComparative health-system profiles and policy analysis.
  • OECD — Health at a GlanceCross-country indicators on health status, systems and performance.

Research methods & statistics
